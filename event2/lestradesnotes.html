Investigation Dossier CONFIDENTIAL

A Soldier's Final Retreat

Investigating Officer: G. Lestrade
Suspect in Custody: Dr. J. H. Watson
"The facts speak for themselves! Every witness confirms the timeline. Watson was enraged, armed with his standard issue revolver, and left alone in the library. All other suspects possess verified alibis."
— G. Lestrade
Witness 01 // Sterling Household

Lady Persimma Sterling

“I was with Archie in the kitchen. Chef had left already so we were putting together some cold meats and cheese for the boys. It seemed like they had a lot to catch up on, and it was going to be a long night, so I thought they would appreciate some light refreshments.”
Lestrade: Irrefutable alibi! In kitchen alongside Archibald. Neither was in library when the trigger was pulled. Eliminates both!
Witness 02 // Son of the House

Archibald Sterling

“I was in the kitchen. Mother had asked me to help prepare some food for the guests. Not sure why Chef couldn’t have done it. Most of the preparation for tonight was already done, so making a small light supper wouldn’t have been too much trouble for him. All our staff seem a little lazy to me. I mean, mother even had to go and take the food to the library. We have a maid to do that. Where was she?”
Lestrade: Corroborates his mother completely. The staff being dismissed gave Watson the isolated wing he required to carry out the deed undisturbed!
Witness 03 // Veteran Officer

Silas Thorne

“I used to have a lot of respect for that man. He was my commanding officer after all. But I don’t know, seeing that photo of us all together, happy, and then finding out that he knew the Afghans were coming, it all changed. It’s as if that happy memory ceased to exist. I’ve never seen Watson that angry. Even on the battlefield. But I think he was angry on my behalf. I had to take him outside to calm down. And he did. Once I’d explained that I’d accepted that Simon died doing a job he loved, he seemed to be able to accept that too.”
THE SMOKING GUN! Thorne admits: "Never seen Watson that angry." Motive: vengeful bloodlust over the Afghan ambush! Thorne claims he calmed down? Absurd! Watson circled back with his service revolver!
Witness 04 // Manor Maid

Maple Strand

“I only got to meet the soldiers briefly. After dinner when they were all chatting in the library, her ladyship said that what with all the other guests arriving the next day, and how busy it was going to be, I could take the rest of the night off. Well, you don’t need to tell me twice. Not often I get a chance to put my feet up.”
Lestrade: Maid absent, retired to quarters. Explains Archie's confusion over missing servants. Watson knew the floor was deserted.
Witness 05 // Guest

Evelyn Ashgrove

“I probably shouldn’t have left the room when all the arguing started. It must seem a little bit like I’d lit the blue touch paper and retired, but I didn’t know what else to do. They seemed like such gentlemen, so I thought if I left them together to talk it out, all would be resolved. I had no idea they would react the way they did.”
Lestrade: Ashgrove fled because of Watson's terrifying quarrel! Leaving Watson as the last man in the room before the fatal shot! Case closed!

Lestrade's Official Finding & Conclusion

"The weapon recovered is a standard military six-chamber revolver — the very make issued to Dr. John H. Watson during his service. Thorne affirms Watson was consumed with murderous rage over the Afghan massacre. Ashgrove confirms the room was vacated except for the combatants. The Sterling family were occupied in the kitchens. There is no other conclusion admissible: Dr. Watson pulled the trigger."

Verdict: Watson's Guilt Proven
